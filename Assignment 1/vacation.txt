Last month I went to Lonavala and Alibaug with my family and it was honestly pretty great. We stayed at this small resort near the beach in Alibaug and the view was amazing. In Lonavala we did the usual tourist stuff like visiting the viewpoints and eating chikki but it was still fun. The weather wasn't too hot which made everything better. I really enjoyed the vada pav we had at some random stall, probably the best part of the trip lol. Overall it was a good break from all the college stress and I'd definitely go back again sometime.
